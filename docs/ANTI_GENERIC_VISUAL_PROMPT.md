# Mining Away — negative visual prompt and evidence

Research date: October 27, 2026. This is an art-direction and quality contract, **not a method for detecting AI authorship**. The small public sample below does not establish what “most AI games” look like. Judge observable composition, craft, behavior and fit to the owner's brief.

**UI is the owner's highest visual priority.** Use the concrete reference selected in the accompanying UI reference brief as the starting composition: reproduce its useful layout, hierarchy and interaction logic with Mining Away's own text, icons, colors and assets. Do not begin with a generic dashboard and apply another theme. The prohibitions below guide review; they must not replace building and inspecting that concrete UI.

## What the research actually shows

- [Anthropic's Opus 5.5 guide](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/prompt-engineering/prompting-claude-opus-5-5) explicitly describes recurring frontend defaults and says a vague request to avoid an AI appearance can simply produce another default. Its practical recommendation is to name unwanted patterns and refine the result. This concerns frontends generally; applying it to Roblox is our design inference.
- [Pocket Tactics' firsthand Roblox Build test](https://www.pockettactics.com/roblox/ai-build) produced a neon farm with six plots and NPCs, and condensed the requested open city into a building. The journalist had asked for cyberpunk, so the neon is **not evidence that AI independently defaults to neon**. It does illustrate why the requested world scope and a generated first pass need comparison.
- Two [Ropilot-published community examples](https://ropilot.ai/) were visually inspected in the browser. The [cottage scene](https://ropilot.ai/media/testimonials/mittens-island.webp) has an isolated small grass base, a fantasy cottage, and separate tree/rock/chest assets lined up beside it. This is an asset presentation, not evidence of a failed playable map; it is a useful negative reference for treating a display diorama as Mining Away's connected world. The [Garden Tools shop](https://ropilot.ai/media/testimonials/p10.webp) has a broad pastel panel, decorative floral corners, a large illustrated title and many item tiles. It is coherent for its theme, but its ornament and screen coverage are wrong for the requested compact mining UI. These two examples themselves show that AI-assisted work has no single appearance.
- A [Roblox developer discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/robloxgamedev/comments/1x6yhhh/theres_so_many_people_using_ai_generated_ui_and/) objects to repetitive UI, while replies emphasize responsiveness/readability. This is anecdotal preference, not a survey or proof of anyone's authorship. Do not repeat its prevalence claims as statistics.

Floating trees, detached/cut-off beams, stale particles and snapping animation are **project-specific defects to prevent**, not research-established signatures of AI. The technical basis is the original parity contract and [Roblox's Beam guide](https://create.roblox.com/docs/effects/beams): beams use two attachments and can vary with camera-facing and graphics quality settings. Inspect the actual render instead of guessing from code.

## Paste-ready negative prompt

> Do not ship a generic generated-looking world, an asset showcase, or a themed dashboard covering the game. Make every major shape, material, animation and interface choice belong to the original Mining Away world. Apply the prohibitions below as observable review criteria, not as a claim to identify how other games were made.
>
> **UI FIRST:** Start from the selected real-game reference layout and interaction, adapted to three choices and this game's identity. No generic card dashboard, default blue-purple gradient, frosted-glass cards, oversized padding or headings, random emoji icons, or the same rounded border/shadow on every element. No giant debug panel, permanent wall of cards or stack of unrelated controls. Make a compact purposeful HUD and temporary three-card offer with consistent spacing, concrete target/stat text, clear focus/press states and generous touch targets. Keep most of the world visible; small must not mean microscopic text. Use one coherent authored visual language and selective accent color. Inspect the actual result against the reference before adding decoration.
>
> **Map:** No faceted low-poly landscape replacement, cone-tree forest, disconnected floating islands, generic cottage/chest diorama, evenly scattered filler rocks, identical trees repeated at fixed intervals, or empty giant platforms dressed with random props. No floating tree trunks, hovering roots, intersecting stairs, buried doors, z-fighting ground layers, or paths that lead nowhere. Build readable connected routes with Roblox Parts, broad simple ground shapes, grounded blocky trees, useful landmarks and deliberate negative space. Keep original authored meshes where they carry identity, especially the crystal silhouette and segmented pyramid turret. Do not erase original assets to comply with a blanket primitive-only rule.
>
> **Materials and lighting:** No all-neon environment, rainbow-everywhere palette, glossy plastic on every surface, excessive bloom, fog hiding weak modeling, or cinematic photorealism pasted over a retro parts world. Use a limited material/palette language from the existing game; reserve bright emission for live resources, machinery and danger. Preserve readable silhouettes under ordinary gameplay lighting.
>
> **RNG interaction:** No fake roulette near-misses, invented odds, full-screen flash, obligatory long spin, endless card bouncing or confetti for every ordinary result. One tactile opening, three server-persisted choices, a readable comparison, intentional selection and a short authoritative acknowledgment. Include fast reveal/skip and reduced-motion behavior. Do not imply timing a click changes the random offer.
>
> **Beams and particles:** No solid oversized neon rods, detached origins, impact points suspended above a shrinking rock, abrupt accidental cutoff before the target, camera-angle disappearance, stale beams after recall/death, sparks from empty space, or identical full-strength effects for charging, mining, fracture and depletion. Use the original narrow inner beam plus restrained outer layer. Render continuously from the current emitter to the visible target surface; verify endpoint transforms, texture and camera-facing behavior. Normal world occlusion is not an endpoint defect. Keep particles short-lived and anchored to the real interaction; make the three fractures and final break readable without obscuring the crystal. Preserve clarity when other players' fleets are active.
>
> **Motion:** No teleporting drones, double-smoothed lag, frame-dependent speed, twitching yaw, nose-diving mining posture, simultaneous mechanical bobbing, identical elastic ease on every element, or static trees that accidentally drift. Preserve original eased/capped travel, small phased hover, level yaw and exact overhead mining. Use purposeful anticipation and release for turret shots and card selection; stop/reverse/cancel transitions cleanly when state changes.
>
> **Completion:** Do not substitute adjectives, a source-code audit, passing lint, fake mockups or one flattering screenshot for looking at the game. Spawn an independent reviewer who must reject unresolved cheap/generic presentation and every concrete defect above. The implementer cannot overrule the reviewer merely because code works. Show real player-height route traversal; full pack interaction at desktop and narrow/mobile layout; moving closeups of warmup, all fracture stages and beam cleanup; low/high graphics settings; and a representative two-player fleet scene. Each failure needs a location, capture/timecode, reason and fix, followed by another review. If the required rendering environment is unavailable, continue useful work and record visual approval as pending. Never mark the goal done without the required visual evidence and reviewer approval.

## Practical review test

Use a normal gameplay camera, with the HUD enabled. Can a new player identify home, safe mining and the next guarded route? Can they watch the original drone and crystal behavior without overlays or particles hiding it? Can they compare all three cards without squinting? Are every tree and stair grounded, and every active beam connected through motion and interruption? Does the scene still have a memorable Mining Away identity when effects are quiet? Any “no” becomes a concrete revision, not an excuse to add more decoration.
