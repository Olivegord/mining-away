# Drones and logistics: research and review notes

Researched November 3, 2026 for the requested special mining drones, four-slot carriers, backpack hauler, and storage launches. The sources below were fetched from Roblox's official `Roblox/creator-docs` repository, rather than inferred from search snippets. Repository requirements in `OWNER_REQUIREMENTS.md`, `ORIGINAL_FEEL_PARITY.md`, `PROGRESSION_AND_ACCEPTANCE.md`, and `MINING_SATISFACTION.md` were also reviewed. Integration targets the current Drone Blower revolution source and verified live capture, preserving the approved map, textured UI, roles, audio, physical ore, and persistence manager. The historical cloud-1 build is not the release base.

## Useful additions grounded in this game's behavior

**Preserve the existing crystal regrowth.** The rejected cloud-1 `seed` loop replenished a patch only when its entire population reached zero. The current live foundation already has individual replacement queues, two-second growth, terrain-aware placement, and geode population accounting. Preserve those systems; they support unattended carriers without another resource rewrite. Geode children must not each become an additional permanent population slot, or repeated shell breaks will inflate the patch.

**Clear launch readiness and automatic work resumption.** A full-storage indicator belongs beside the storage meter and on the launch building. Workers waiting for capacity should keep their valid patch order and resume after a launch creates room. This removes repetitive re-commanding while preserving the requested player-controlled launch. It does not require automatically launching cargo without a visible setting.

These are recommendations derived from the repository's concrete resource and assignment behavior. They are not claims that a retention study or player experiment has validated them.

## Edge cases to verify

- **Near-full cargo:** the current card/shop capacity helper starts the backpack at 40 units before upgrades; orbital storage starts at 120 units. A typed reward may not fit into the last few free units. Refusing it while waiting for cargo to become exactly full creates a deadlock. Preserve overflow and mineral identity in the run's pending cargo. The same issue applies to full-only storage launches. Ordinary miners retain the existing finite-lifetime physical ore pickups; carrier workers send their rewards into typed storage instead.
- **No lost stored ore:** moving collected cargo from a player to a backpack, from a backpack to storage, or from storage to an ascending capsule must conserve the total and each mineral type. Partial deposits must leave the remainder in a defined owner. Launch deposits each mineral into its matching existing shop wallet, preserving the game's currency model and verified sale progress. The same capsule must not pay twice after a repeated command, disconnect, autosave, or restart. Uncollected physical ore still follows its previously approved finite lifetime and fade.
- **Mining cancellation:** one-shot completion must recheck life, current target, reservation, health, and patch permission. Recall, death, target removal, or encounter reset must cancel the previous operation and its visuals.
- **Shared geodes:** the original shell allows three miners, while one resource-level effect record describes only one special animation. Use an explicit exclusivity rule or ordinary shell mining so simultaneous specials do not overwrite each other's visual intent.
- **Replication:** attributes are not an atomic multi-property transaction. A client must wait or retry if type, start time, duration, or serial is not yet available. Late joins should show current progress without replaying historical payouts.
- **Distant carriers:** leaving a patch must not stop its server simulation or remove the player's ability to recall its carrier when the model streams out.
- **Bounded effects:** visual fragments must be non-queryable and non-colliding, with a global cosmetic budget and deterministic cleanup. Their motion never authorizes damage or rewards.
- **Economy writes:** overlapping autosave, purchase, departure, and shutdown saves must not write an older snapshot after a newer one. Lease loss must prevent further permanent mutations being reported as saved.

## Assumptions and remaining evidence

Drone costs, cycle durations, 120-unit storage capacity, and launch timing are initial tuning choices. The smaller capsule preserves access to the existing 40-Quartz pack and 160-sale-earnings first prestige more closely than a 600-unit first capsule. Official API documentation establishes engineering constraints, not the best balance. Useful balance measurements include time to the first launch and first special drone, ordinary-rock throughput by type, carrier output before storage fills, and time spent waiting for a backpack trip. The source catalog is authoritative for current prices and work/yield tradeoffs; arithmetic comparisons are not player pacing measurements.

Official Luau release **0.745** was downloaded from [luau-lang/luau](https://github.com/luau-lang/luau/releases/tag/0.745) into `/tmp/luau` for compilation, analysis, and deterministic tests. The toolchain is outside the repository.

Compilation, standalone Luau rules, and API-double integration tests cannot establish Roblox engine appearance, real DataStore durability, networked multiplayer behavior, streaming correctness, mobile glass readability, or measured gameplay pacing. Those require Studio or live test sessions, including two clients, late joins, a distant deployed carrier, a launch interrupted by reconnect, and repeated mine/recall/reprint cycles. Final validation should report which of these were actually executed.
