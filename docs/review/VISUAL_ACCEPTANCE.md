# Mining Away visual acceptance and independent veto

The current owner direction is a deliberately authored, retro Roblox **parts-built alien world**, with compact readable earned-currency card choices and clear original drone/mineral/sentry identities. Bee Swarm Simulator is a reference for connected fields, visible destination hierarchy and physical landmarks; its assets and theme are not to be copied. Original drone, crystal union and segmented construct meshes remain protected.

## Review authority and status

The independent visual art director has **veto authority**. An implementation, screenshot mock, source review, large diff, passing automated tests, generated schematic or visually attractive still does not constitute approval. The reviewer must inspect the actual final candidate build in motion; if the final engine evidence is absent, the verdict is **NOT VERIFIED**. Any hard defect below means **FAIL**, followed by a specific repair and new final-revision evidence. A developer's disagreement cannot change the reviewer's verdict to approval. Keep the existing PR draft and visually unapproved until cleared.

Critique observable craft. Do not infer that an image was generated by AI from its pixels or claim that a defect appears in 'most' AI games without a representative study.

## Hard rejection criteria and authored alternatives

| Reject on final in-engine inspection | Require instead |
|---|---|
| Interchangeable faceted islands, repetitive cone forests, giant unstructured empty floor, random prop scatter, or broad same-colored cliff wallpaper. | A bounded connected landscape made from intentional blocks/slabs, with a dominant destination, readable field edges, material/color hierarchy, and one distinctive silhouette per district. Use placed repetition as a deliberate construction language. |
| Broken paths that form comb-like slivers, gaps, overlapping strips or visually disconnected entrances. | Clearly joined continuous retro paths with deliberate straight, corner and junction pieces; traversable field entrances and readable route choices. |
| Trees or props hovering, buried, clipping, or visually detached from terrain, including roots on slopes and paths. | Explicit grounded bases and root/foot contact with the actual local surface; check side views and traversal after every terrain change. |
| Full-screen dashboard panels, three giant repetitive buttons, generic rainbow/gradient stacks, decorative icon mismatches or illegible tiny tradeoffs. | A compact physical-feeling three-choice reveal that keeps the world visible, respects viewport/input, shows exact named/type target, actual benefit/drawback and run scope, and provides one unambiguous interaction. |
| Cosmetic rarity that implies nonexistent odds, fake near-miss animations, visual rerolls or cues that contradict an already saved offer. | Reveal only the three server-committed choices with a brief, interruptible, consistent tactile presentation. Reopening preserves the same options and cost; use role/rarity labels only when truthfully supported. |
| Muddy, arbitrary neon/bloom that overwhelms targets; beams clipped/truncated by geometry or textures. | A narrow readable core and restrained halo, clear muzzle and impact, bounded width/brightness, intact texture across normal and close cameras. |
| Beam origins or impact endpoints detached from a moving drone/turret or a shrinking/tilting crystal, including fork/chain effects. | Update endpoints against current rendered emitters and the current visible surface, handle loss/retarget/death cleanly, and remove an effect if its target cannot be verified. |
| Opaque particle squares, particles through surfaces, excessive/noisy density/color, abrupt emissions or pops. | Small finite intentionally directed particles and grounded impacts, with unobtrusive fades; verify appearance at both low and high graphics settings. |
| Snapping, jitter, broken easing, object clipping, invalid transitions, or missed/lost mining stages. | Original-like eased horizontal movement, smooth level yaw, continuous attachment, stable life/target changes, two-second growth and all three readable fracture transitions before break. |
| Visual clutter obscuring a co-op battle, infinite effects or effects lingering after targets disappear. | Bounded effects/connections and readable busy-scene silhouettes; immediate cleanup on death, despawn, recall, reprint and lost target. |

## Required evidence of the exact final commit

1. An uncut, normal-speed **player-height moving video** of drone launch, travel/hover, active beam, all three crystal stages, break, retarget and free reprint. Include closeups and multiple camera angles; inspect moving muzzle and impact contact against the actual shrinking/tilting surface. Repeat at low and high graphics settings. A single still cannot establish these checks.
2. Player-height continuous traversal from depot through each safe and guarded route, including junctions, steps, slopes, tree/prop bases and field boundaries. Include orbit/side views that expose hovering/buried roots and path slivers. Review a representative busy multi-player scene.
3. Actual earned three-choice opening, short reveal, hover, keyboard focus, tap, selection, close/reopen and reconnect on desktop and narrow phone viewports. Show exact target/effect/tradeoff and fixed offer; no UI fakes or clipped text.
4. Two real clients plus a late join during mining, battle and reprint, including observer beam origins, visual states and busy-scene legibility. Verify one coherent server battle and no stale or ghost effects.
5. Identify the exact candidate commit/build checksum and graphics/device settings for every recording. Log findings by scene, timestamp, concrete defect, severity, repair and retest evidence. The reviewer independently inspects fresh final-revision evidence and gives APPROVE / FAIL / NOT VERIFIED.

Automated geometry, timing, ownership/persistence, anti-attrition and effect-lifecycle checks remain necessary; they test logic, not appearance. Outside-engine model renders, old screenshots, API doubles and simulations are explicitly supplemental and cannot close the visual gate. Do not publish Roblox or merge the draft PR as a substitute for acceptance.
