# Quarry composition follow-up

The owner rejected the v39 environment for its scale, muddy surfaces, weak composition and inconspicuous lights. This pass changes the scenery around the existing six mining fields and routes. Ore geometry and gameplay are preserved.

Cobalt has a dominant 58-stud blue trumpet, a 44-stud supporting crown and a branching coral fan. Curved, tapered stalks lead into broad ribbed undersides, with sparse luminous gills. The grove's pale ground and cool stone distinguish it from surrounding greenery. Ruby has taller overlapping gray stone plates, scarlet ground cover and a few glowing veins. Emerald has a full narrow stone crossing through one terrain lagoon, taller broken white cliffs and layered foliage on its ledges. Warm hooded lanterns stand at the hub and along existing approaches.

The low-resolution photo and fog work is integrated with the active lighting chat. Read the texture credits and current release record for final asset IDs, native settings and publication evidence.

The owner's later patterned-world reference clarified the need for different pattern sizes and structures. Four plaza walking surfaces use 64-pixel herringbone brick from Poly Haven. The three small service pads use worn checker tiles from ambientCG. The larger hub floor, mining beds and biome surfaces keep their own quieter finishes. These two CC0 sources introduce architectural patterns without reproducing the reference's cloth, stars, dice or flower prints. Credits are in `assets/quarry-textures/README.md`.

## Verification and limits

One Studio Play session was used to inspect the previous scene and preview the new canopy scale, gills, cliff composition, crossing and lanterns. The proposed 42–300 stud fog washed out distant landmarks; the longer-distance comparison retained more contrast. The first helper used the wrong base material for the foliage variant; this was corrected and the textured leaves were verified in the same session. Play then ended externally and was not restarted by this chat.

The tapered stem mesh, branching secondary coral shelves and smaller foreground shore-tree crowns were compiled after that stop and inspected in the actual authored Edit scene. That view identified pink Ruby ground and square cliff tops; the bounded follow-up changes the photo grade to scarlet and adds jagged crowns. A coordinate/native-terrain check extended the crossing to the far bank, with 31 collidable slabs. The production and Studio core-loop suites passed using API doubles. These checks do not establish owner visual acceptance, mobile performance, multiplayer appearance or collision feel. The latest publication and exact installed revision are recorded in `docs/PROJECT_STATE.md`.
