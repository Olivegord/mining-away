# In-use effects and economy review

Reviewed the actual gameplay renderers against the home demo, plus the authoritative mining abilities, eight hotbar tools, pack eligibility and all five mineral counters. This change starts from the connected quarry biomes and retains their map and photo materials.

| Family | What the review changed or checked |
| --- | --- |
| Regular miner and combat | Kept the verified surface contact, thin laser, regular color cuts and launch overlap. Checked the existing motion and beam envelope suites. |
| Bubbler, Halver, Gridcutter, Singularity, Starforge, Frostbite, Resonator | Gameplay now carries the warmup rig into the first work gesture instead of disposing of it on the first specialist frame. The demo uses the same handoff. Halver ejects two fitted pieces. Gridcutter cuts on the actual rotated rock surface, ejects nine textured blocks and adds a short pressure ring. The final cut breaks the whole remaining body; partial cuts preserve the grounded rock. Enlarged the gravity, ice and resonance circles relative to the target. |
| Breaker, Grower, Healer, Courier and Scrap | Breaker warning, touchdown and fracture circles scale with the rock. Checked the existing distinct seeding, repair, parcel transfer and confirmed scrap chisel cues. The gadget buttons now select only reachable, unlocked and cleared fields. |
| Orbital | Equipment, specialist and drop all get the same 2.4-second aiming period. A thin red line and contracting ring share the target. The active beam reaches the ground; contact sparks follow the rock top. An initial ground wave and a smaller contact wave scale with the rock. Damage, reach, four-second work budget, and saved cooldowns are server-owned. |
| Bowling, Crowbar, Jelly, Sword, Sledgehammer and Molten Tongs | Visible rubble uses the hit rock's material and size where available. The sword adds a cut flash and six fitted pieces from its detached volume. Hammer and ball have a surface pressure wave. Jelly has a ground wave at its blast range and distinct gel droplets. Molten tongs have hot droplets and a smaller contact ring. Interrupted temporary Orbital and Scrap deployments return the item if no hit was accepted. |
| Packs and shops | Early packs omit guard-only upgrades until the player can prepare for an uncleared guard. Chain and repair-on-kill cards wait for a reachable multi-sentry field. Tradeoff cards are labeled for the benefit they confer. Existing saved choices stay intact. Cobalt's former pack discount now also gives 25% ore yield per level under the same saved upgrade ID. Packs stay free. Counters block purchases that do nothing for the current fleet at its caps, explain role requirements and show the amount of currency still needed. |
