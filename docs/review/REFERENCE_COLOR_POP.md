# Original-reference color pop and longer launch sequence

The owner rejected the brief blue-tinted pulses and the first attempted restoration, saying the color pop had lost its point. Reinspection of the supplied clip and the preserved reference frames, particularly `build/reference-beam-study/frame-03.75.png`, shows a large, hard distortion silhouette with black regions and saturated color separation. It is not a pastel rainbow glow. Reading the first beam commit also confirmed that 765a107 predates the chromatic lens itself; restoring that file wholesale would not restore the requested pop.

Native comparisons in the existing Play session tested the original Glass/Highlight technique with different distortion and color thresholds. Simply increasing brightness filled the circle with a flat color. Adding a threshold to the retained color channel preserved black regions while increasing contrast saturated the remaining refracted areas. The comparison instances were removed before saving; they were never part of the authored map.

The final lens uses fixed distortion and contrast during each collapse, rather than fading those properties with diameter. A blue pop collapses, a larger red/yellow pop follows, then progressively smaller green/cyan/green pops lead to release. Its maximum regular-drone diameter is 3.25 studs. Discrete masks replace continuous rainbow interpolation. Dust uses six saturated RGB/CMY hues from the visible chromatic output, while smoke and the plane retain the beam color. Smoke emission was reduced for the longer charge so it does not obscure the pop.

The full base charge is now 3.2 seconds instead of 0.8, including the inward swirl. A runtime multiplier in Cards changes effective warmup without rewriting permanent base traits. Startup cards continue to shorten it and active damage is unchanged. This intentionally increases pre-attack downtime. The home preview uses the same 3.2-second charge and keeps its short 0.2-second recovery gap.

The ring retains its rapid initial expansion but continues to expand slowly during a 1.1-second fade, ending at about 2.52 studs radius. Fragments brake into a small continuing outward/downward current with electric kicks and no gravity, remaining visible for 1.25–1.46 seconds. Smoke survives the release for up to 1.8 seconds. All tails retain their independent lifetime and bounded four-rig pool.

Audio now uses [Menacing laser charge by pebblm](https://create.roblox.com/store/asset/139131445232833) and [Laser beam by pebblm](https://create.roblox.com/store/asset/139620337204036), discovered through the connected Creator Store search and loaded successfully in Studio. A continuous rising charge replaces the servo-like pulse ticks. The renderer owns one charge loop per visible charge; the state observer skips its duplicate, and release/cancellation stops the loop. Ignition shares the existing cue cooldown with state receipts. The small 0.028-stud pre-attenuation camera impulse remains.
