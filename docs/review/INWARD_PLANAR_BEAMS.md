# Inward streams and planar release

The owner rejected revision 4bb5a49, requested the integration chat's first beam version, then clarified the desired sequence and shape: immediate inward swirl and suction, laser release with a flat spread, much finer lasers, and no firing hop. Reading the integration chat's original completed turn identifies its first native installation as 765a107. The current implementation restores those smaller beam proportions and removes the later oversized looping sculptures and ball release while preserving the pooling and source-only integration fixes.

`PullMotion` integrates cosmetic point masses under softened central attraction and drag. They start with inward and tangential velocity; there is no outward drifting phase or prescribed orbit loop. Eight deterministic paths are cached at 240 samples per normalized charge and interpolated at authoritative server progress. Radius decreases until capture; trails sample the prior path, so their length and curvature follow motion. This is a local visual simulation, not server physics or gameplay damage.

`PlaneBurst` now forms one circular expanding sheet from four cubic quadrants in the plane perpendicular to the shot. Attachment orientation keeps its width in that plane; it does not face the camera or rotate around the emitter. The Glass/chromatic lens remains confined to the emitter charge. The later large dark loops, orbiting debris and spherical launch flash are gone. The regular/combat body, fine core, support and specialist beams, turret fallback layers and orbital cylinder/ramp were narrowed. The added firing recoil was removed from both production drone flight and the home display. Original small hover and existing recall behavior remain.

The owner's subsequent requests add a persistent launch aftermath. The lens uses three continuous pulses with peak amplitudes 0.72, 1.0 and 0.43; collapse intervals shorten from 0.19 to 0.15 to 0.095 of authoritative charge time. A separate `LaunchAftermath` renderer holds the existing violet smoke through release and applies a short radial acceleration impulse. Eighteen tiny colored cuboids shoot down a cone using cached force-integrated paths with short lateral velocity kicks, gravity and drag. Their positions interpolate at render time; no constraints, collision or server simulation are added. The ring fades over 0.65 seconds, fragments over 0.8–1.19 seconds and smoke over up to 1.8 seconds. Launched effects detach from the moving charge owner so even a short attack ending cannot erase them. Four active rigs and four cached rigs bound the system; it disconnects its render callback when idle and respects distance/reduced-effects culling.

The pressure impulse uses Acceleration and Drag, which affect existing particles; it does not clear and replace the cloud at release. See [Roblox ParticleEmitter documentation](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/ParticleEmitter).
