### Hard findings on that earlier candidate

1. **Overview and spawn orientation — FAIL for the new brief.** `expanded-map-overview.png` shows separated low strips of terrain sitting in a huge unbroken mint expanse. Thin cream paths snake between them, but there is no clear dominant destination or distinctive district silhouette. `expanded-open-hub.png` shows a broad flat empty floor and repeated horizontal teal cliff bands; the actual depot machines are tiny and blend into the ledges. The visual result reads like a generic empty diorama. A new grass texture or extra random props alone will not repair that composition. Compose a purposeful player-height foreground, midground, endpoint and vista; define field boundaries and a distinctive landmark at each decision point, and preserve actual walking/working room.
2. **Road joints — FAIL for sloppy execution in that earlier screenshot.** `expanded-map-overview.png` shows fan/comb-like pale slivers at curved route joints, notably the Ruby entrance and Emerald arc. Those edges resemble overlapping or gapped segments rather than authored slabs. Repair route bends, mitres and ground contact, and inspect their silhouettes from player height and a shallow camera angle. The still does not establish a physical collision failure.
3. **Ruby mine/field — material and hierarchy need repair.** `expanded-ruby-grove.png` contains a recognizable mine entrance, but several red trees share the same blocky crown, broad bare wall sections dominate, and important minerals resemble the faceted scenery. The center purple crystal appears suspended over a translucent band. Inspect the actual moving rock, stage and ground contact before treating that as either a bug or an intentional state. Keep a simple parts-built vocabulary; make field edges and working targets more legible than decoration, while preserving the actual user crystal mesh.
4. **Pack choice — FAIL for the requested compact chance UI in the earlier shot.** `ui-card-choices.png` covers nearly the whole viewport with a giant cream sheet and three tall, similarly weighted dashboard columns. Repeated full-width orange buttons and large blocks of explanatory text leave almost no world view. Static copy is mostly legible, but the image does not demonstrate anticipation, reveal, hover/tap/focus, a saved offer reopening, or tactile timing. Design a smaller, tightly paced physical opening/choice: three clearly distinct resolved items, meaningful restrained focus, exact benefit/target/tradeoff and visible run scope. Do not use a fake near miss, introduce a Robux sale, or hide the choice's material terms. Render/fit both desktop and touch states.
5. **HUD and phone fleet — needs reduction and real interaction review.** `ui-gameplay-hud.png` stacks prominent cream boxes upper-left and a large context bar at the bottom; they compete for attention with the equipment/player. `ui-phone-fleet.png` displays readable text, but nearly all available height becomes a modal with large panels. Check whether the new actual gameplay HUD preserves world visibility and whether touch controls/safe insets overlap. A phone still does not prove touch activation, rotation, or opening/claim/reopening.

The prior screenshots contain no qualifying full-speed final-candidate beam/particle/traversal evidence. Do not call an unobserved bug present or absent based only on source code or a still.

## Final-candidate hard-fail gates

A final approval is impossible if any of the following appear in an actual final-candidate capture:

- A beam mesh/texture clips, truncates, snaps, intermittently disappears, or has a visible gap at its muzzle or impact. Test a moving/yawing drone and an actual crystal shrinking/tilting at 75/50/25%, then a geode, target loss, recall and reprint. The endpoint must track the visible surface in the viewer's camera, not a stale server/full-size center. Overhead drone body remains level.
- Opaque/square particle cards, particles born inside/through a surface, noisy unattractive color/density, visible pooling cutoffs, bloom muddying the core, or abrupt show/hide or impact pops. Inspect full-speed and frame-by-frame at close and ordinary camera distances, under both low and high Roblox graphics. Decorative effects must not conceal rock state, other drones, a telegraph or card terms.
- Hover, flight, aim, target reassignment, card reveal, focus, destruction/reprint, or turret charge/fire/recovery jitter, snap or clip. There must be a readable frozen-aim warning and unambiguous shot/recovery, with effects ending cleanly on death/reset. Meaningful gameplay continues to be server-authoritative.
- A tree root, sign foot, rock support, machine, bridge or other grounded prop visibly hovers, buries itself into a slope, intersects the player's working lane, or casts a clearly detached shadow. Inspect at several inclines and approach angles. Tidy purposeful part joints; no accidental fans/spikes, z-fighting, seams or clipping.
- The map reads as giant empty platforms, low-poly faceted filler, random unrelated prop scatter, identical tree stamps without a district role, disconnected landmarks, incoherent palettes, or washed-out unlabeled navigation. A large map is acceptable only when normal player-height composition and routes are legible. Preserve the actual original drone/crystal and segmented pyramid silhouette.
- The pack is a giant dashboard or stock rainbow rarity pile; timing misrepresents a real saved outcome; hover/tap/focus is absent, unresponsive, or jumps; card choice text, price, exact recipient/scope, tradeoff, run expiry, pending state or outcome clips or misleads. Player must be able to close/reopen the exact paid saved choices, select one, and understand the result. Inspection includes narrow portrait/landscape, safe areas, keyboard/gamepad and real touch where available.
