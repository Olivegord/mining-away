At the same camera/scale compare idle radius-10/+7 formation, 30+ stud assignment, eased final approach, level yaw, directly-overhead +10 mining station, laser show/hide, grow-in, all three 75/50/25% stages and recall. Walk every route and ramp, inspect depot/tree/field scale and signs, and ensure the compact HUD leaves the drones visible. Record observed first-pack time; the model's 55 seconds is not a playtest result.
3. **Pack durability:** at the machine open one paid pack. Confirm three distinct useful choices, exact named/type targets, actual stat changes and This Run. Close/reopen and reconnect in Preview's same running server: same offer, no extra debit. Race two selections: one modifier only. 4. **Pyramid and skill:** challenge Cobalt without combat cards, observe frozen targeting rays and Q flank evade. Repeat with a combat/hull build and record actual outcome. 5. **Free reprint:** observe destruction, visible home reconstruction and countdown on both clients; identity, type, base traits and current-run cards remain. No dead reservations, beams or ghost damage. A speed card should not reroll hover phase.
6. **Prestige and second run:** earn the visible cumulative-sales/Cobalt milestone, resolve a pending pack, recall own combat and confirm the exact reset at the beacon. Keep Blue ID/base, add one Red, lose all old run modifiers/cargo/credits/clearance, and complete another pack/mining/guarded-field cycle with both. Duplicate/stale prestige and old-offer requests must not duplicate drones or reapply cards. A friend's fight/clearance must survive your prestige.
7. **Late join:** connect during crystal growth, mining, geode opening, a turret charge and reprint. All observers see complete original drone art, comparable flight, correct surface impacts/stages and current state without replayed rewards. Outsiders cannot mine another run's cleared expedition or join its locked assault. Repeating this across two local clients is still required even though API-double snapshots pass.
8. **UI/touch/performance:** inspect three-card selection at 320/360/720/desktop widths and after device rotation. Read long target IDs/effects, choose, scroll, inspect fleet, use native station prompts and compact controls. Test six visible drones, several players, repeated respawns and leave/rejoin; no accumulated viewmodels, connection leaks, chips or editor-animation clone history. Record actual frame/network performance.
