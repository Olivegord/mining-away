# Studio acceptance checklist

These checks are pending. The cloud executor had no Roblox Studio engine. Use the NEW copy, never overwrite/publish the live place for this review.

1. Open `mining-away-cloud.rbxl`. Confirm no corruption/unknown property dialog. Play and inspect Output. The original map and templates should remain; a home platform and three connected patches appear above the original spawn. Confirm the reused drone/crystal pivots, scale and beam origins. Walk every bridge, including both joins, and verify jumps are not required.
2. Verify the historical loading screen disappears, normal character camera follows and the HUD remains usable at your screen size. Recall, Sell, Power and Hull buttons must respond. Respawn and confirm old UI/camera controllers do not return.
3. At Ruby, order mining. Ordinary crystals must show at most one beam; shell at most three. Drone travel/hover/aim, beam core/glow, impact sparks and local break sound should look intentional. Shell fractures should build in three stages, then expose a core and four small crystals. Confirm no shell payment and exactly one payout per child. Watch ore capacity: a full fleet returns without exceeding 2000. Return home to sell; attempt sale while away and confirm no inventory mutation.
4. Start a local server with **two clients**. Client A mines Ruby while B observes; both should see identical drone positions, all beams, targets and breaks. B cannot command A's drones or sell A's ore. Examine server Workspace and Player attributes rather than client-only counters.
5. With A already mining, connect **another client late**. It must immediately see existing drones, beams, partially mined shell HP/fracture, turret HP/phase and reprint models. Repeat late join during an active assault and during a clear window. Joining mid-assault must not permit attacking or mining defenses/resources.
6. Both clients approach Cobalt. A starts; B joins during the four-second muster. Check both fleets are enrolled and every shot has a visible charge. Place temporary server-owned test walls between turret and drone; neither side should damage through the wall. Remove the wall. Verify cooldowns and HP bars from both clients.
7. Attempt Amber with zero upgrades. Confirm deaths clear reservations/beams, printer countdown is 12 seconds total with visible final two-second assembly, and replacements originate at home. Ore, credits, owned fleet size and upgrades remain unchanged. New replacements must not rejoin a friend's active battle.
8. Test staggered deaths: let B keep one fleet alive while A repeatedly dies/reprints. A's replacements cannot join the roster. Kill one turret, then wipe/recall all fleets; **all turrets**, including the destroyed one, restore. No active/reset mining or ghost reservations should remain. Repeat retreat into home healing and re-entry; those slots must remain spent. Disconnect/reconnect A while B fights: A's new models cannot reinforce.
9. Keep a drone in range without winning for 90 seconds; the hard timeout must reset the full patch. Remove all eligible drones and observe immediate wipe reset; no eight-second timer should leave damage behind. Verify telegraphs disappear and the six-second reset cooldown cannot be skipped.
10. Buy level 1 Power/Hull and defeat Cobalt; then level 3 Power/Hull for Amber. Confirm a single clear bonus per enrolled connected user, no per-turret payments, and global 120-second mining access. Let the window expire during mining: resources/reservations reset and fleets recall. Confirm exhausted resources regrow after 12 seconds only while the patch is mineable.
11. From a disposable client test script, send NaN/infinity/table/string/fractional patch IDs, unknown actions, another Player as the Sell payload and an input flood. Confirm no errors or arbitrary rewards. Modify local model HP/attributes and verify server simulation/cargo do not change.
12. Exercise repeated character respawns, leave/rejoin and two clients leaving together. Inspect for orphaned models, beams, stale reservations or old timers. Monitor server/client performance with a representative player count. Bound cosmetics should not accumulate. Check audio permissions and mute/adjust ambience if necessary.

If the binary does not open, retain the original and include the exact error. `tools/build.py` reproducibly patches only Source/Disabled chunks; source and original are supplied for recovery. No engine-load or multiplayer acceptance result is claimed by the cloud tests.
